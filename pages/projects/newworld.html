New World Creative Studio - Site Build and Design

Web Development, Design, UX Design, Web Design, Photography, Illustration.

Welcome to my pride and joy! This is my most ambitious web build to date — designed, developed, and coded entirely from scratch. I created it to reflect the full creative and technical range of the studio, from visual identity through to user experience and interactive design.
I designed my site with a variety of advanced technical features to showcase the work that I can do for my clients. The site uses custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no pre-built templates. I incorporated features such as parallax scrolling, hover-triggered image galleries, and micro-interactions to keep the experience dynamic and sleek.


The front page features text that shifts in the direction of on the mouse hover. This also translates onto mobile with thumb. The ham menu also changes the font color to white against a hot pink background when toggled.

I have made the parallax scroll effect a main tool on the first page to showcase the skills offered. There is a rectangle feature - made the same dimensions as the poster, that scrolls faster. Plus images which scroll at different speeds to create a 3 dimensional feel.

I have created a perspective shift effect on the case studies page. The mouse also converts into a button that can be scrolled over the relevant images. On mobile, the perspective shift of the images occurrs on scroll.

Throughout the site, I used a hover effect where the elements change colour and an arrow appears. The use of the arrow in North East direction reflects the idea of a "new world" reflected in the navigation on the site.
