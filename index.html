The Founder
Isabelle Johnson, CCO

I’m Isabelle Johnson, multilingual founder New World Creative Studio, a creative studio helping to build brands and businesses nationally and internationally.   

I’m passionate about creating work that is innovative and full of character. I have a flare for the exuberance of high fashion and I am able to translate this style into imagery and creative work that people want to portray and create for their brand.

I do this in tiers for your brand, creating bespoke experiential websites, with bespoke web content and photography.

Led by me, I sometimes bring in other freelancers including back end engineers and designers depending on project needs. I've worked with fashion brands like Vogue France, to international ad agencies and IKEA and I bring a diverse creative perspective and an unconventional approach to every collaboration, creating a bespoke creative package for your brand.

I understand that a beautiful digital experience is another piece of communication, through brilliant copy, visuals and technical features that differentiate your brand and work.
